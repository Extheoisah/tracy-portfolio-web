Mavapay — Simplifying payments for local integration

Designing a lightning-fast payment rail interface and unified B2B dashboard to bridge local currencies with digital Lightning infrastructure across Nigeria.

01 // The challenge

Bridging the gap between instant Lightning settlement and local currency realities.

Traditional cross-border payments in Nigeria suffer from high fees, multi-day settlement delays, and high failure rates. While Bitcoin’s Lightning Network offers instant, virtually free global rails, local merchants do not want the volatility of holding crypto. Our challenge was to design a platform where businesses could receive local currency (NGN) settled instantly via global liquidity pools, hiding all technical blockchain complexity under a seamless, familiar bank transfer experience.

“We had to design an experience that felt less like a complex crypto wallet and more like the reliable banking tools merchants already use every day.” — Tracy Akhionbare, Lead Product Designer

02 // The process

Validating with local shop owners, defining our design guidelines.

We initiated our research on-ground in Lagos, interviewing 18 local merchants ranging from tech startups to local trade wholesalers. The primary anxiety was trust: “How do I know my money is safe and when will it reach my local bank account?” This led us to develop three key design pillars: absolute transparency in pricing rates, instant visual confirmation, and a robust status feedback mechanism.

Unified Merchant Analytics Portal

A clean, responsive console for business owners to monitor real-time transactions, download financial ledgers, and manage team API permissions.

Customer-Facing Payment Experience

High fidelity interactive flow ensuring customers can easily scan Lightning QR codes, view clear conversion rates, and finalize NGN payouts seamlessly.

03 // Results & metrics

Reducing settlement friction to zero, empowering local trade.

Following the system implementation, transaction success rate shot up to 99.4%, while payment settlement time went from standard 48 hours down to under 5 seconds. Merchants registered an average 18% reduction in currency exchange fees, giving local enterprises the freedom to re-invest their margins directly into inventory and business scale.
